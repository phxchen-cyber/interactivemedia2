I read "Game Design UX Best Practices" by Amir Dori. I've read about dark patterns in UX/UI before and encountering this article was a refresher on all the different ways a designer could impliment design decisions to achieve certain goals.

From what I gather from this article, there are so many different methods to increase a game's KPI. I wonder how it would differ when it's a desktop game instead of a mobile one. The finger becomes a cursor and the user is no longer more likely to interact with certain areas of a screen. What methods are no longer effective in those scenarios?
